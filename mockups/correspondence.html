NEAR / FAR

Correspondence, 06

Two coastlines. One eye.

Your archive has an unusual strength: local aerial work and years of European travel can speak to each other. Instead of organising only by place, this direction pairs photographs by shape, colour, gesture and atmosphere.

It makes the portfolio feel authored rather than filed. Each visit becomes a visual essay, while every image still leads back to its location, map and print edition.

Sam Duckworth Photography
