Sam Duckworth Photography — concept round

EIGHT DIRECTIONS

Eight reimaginings of samduckworth.com, all running on your real work — a curated set pulled from your 589 published frames, with the real titles, GPS and drone altitudes straight out of Supabase, served off the same image CDN the live site uses. Same photos in every concept, so the only thing that changes is the design. Open one, then use the switcher pinned at the bottom to flick between them.

How these were made. Design system generated with the ui-ux-pro-max skill (84 UI styles, 192 palettes, 74 font pairings, 98 UX rules) — it scored "Exaggerated Minimalism" + "Portfolio Grid" + gallery-black palette highest for a fine-art photography portfolio, and those recommendations are the backbone of 01 and 02. 03–05 push past the database into interaction concepts.
Nothing here touches the live site.
